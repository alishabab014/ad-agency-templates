Questions

Before you ask, we'll answer.

Why should my business hire a Google Ads agency?

Google Ads rewards accounts that are structured, tested and optimized correctly from day one — mistakes are expensive because they're paid for in real time. An agency brings the platform experience and reporting discipline to spend efficiently instead of learning on your budget.

How quickly can Google Ads generate results?

Search campaigns can start driving qualified traffic within days of launch. Reliable performance data — enough to make scaling decisions — typically takes 2 to 4 weeks.

What's your minimum ad spend?

We typically work with accounts spending $2,000 a month or more. Below that, most platforms can't gather enough data fast enough to optimize meaningfully.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Most clients start on a 3-month initial term to get through the testing phase, then move to month-to-month.

Who owns the ad account and the data?

You do. Accounts are built under your own Google Ads login, and you keep full access if the engagement ever ends.
